# Wine Cellar Page Design

## Goal

Add a dedicated `/wine-cellar` landing page for the premium private wine cellar concept described in `тз-винный-погреб.md`.

## Direction

- Aesthetic: `Quiet Luxury Cellar`
- Tone: discreet, precise, status-driven, anti-retail
- Emotional anchor: a protected private world where provenance and capital are defended with the same rigor as atmosphere and service

## Page Structure

1. Hero
   - Private wine mansion positioning
   - Status message and private-visit CTA
2. Trust Architecture
   - Sensor data, provenance, TCA protection
3. Private Visit
   - No public traffic, no random encounters, client-first advisory
4. Advisory Philosophy
   - Family-office style support rather than sales pressure
5. Rare Selection
   - Burgundy allocations, Bordeaux investment lots, non-retail framing
6. Guarantee Manifesto
   - No-risk stance on defects and authenticity concerns
7. Collector Dashboard
   - Liv-ex integration and portfolio management framing
8. Services / Experts / Testimonials
   - Turn the concept into an operating trust system
9. Private Contact Form
   - Reservation and request for selection or storage

## Visual Notes

- Dark obsidian, graphite, warm brass, and deep cellar-wine tones
- Architectural frames and dashboard-like panels
- Sparse but elegant motion
- Mansion references in the hero and atmosphere rather than a storefront layout
