# Bathhouse Finance Section Design

## Goal

Add a new finance section to `/bathhouse` that turns the page into a more complete investor-facing presentation without replacing the already approved strategic narrative above it.

## Direction

- Format: `Executive Finance Section`
- Role on page: a new final section placed after `Vision / Investor Note`
- Tone: calm, premium, presentation-first, and analytical without looking like a spreadsheet

## Content Structure

1. Intro
   - Heading: `Экономика банного комплекса`
   - Short framing text that explains the project as a high-ticket recovery asset with controlled operating model
2. KPI Row
   - Monthly expenses
   - Forecast revenue
   - Operating margin
   - Net profit
3. Expense Model
   - Payroll and staffing logic
   - Consumables, marketing, amortization, and reserve
4. Revenue Structure
   - Rental without rituals
   - Rental with rituals
   - SPA services
   - Food and beverage
5. Operating Logic
   - What utilization assumptions the model rests on
   - Why the format keeps premium pricing and repeat demand defensible

## Presentation Notes

- No Excel-style tables
- Numbers should be readable in 2 to 3 seconds
- Supporting copy should stay compact and investor-facing
- Keep the visual language aligned with `/wine-cellar` finance treatment, but slightly warmer and more wellness-oriented
